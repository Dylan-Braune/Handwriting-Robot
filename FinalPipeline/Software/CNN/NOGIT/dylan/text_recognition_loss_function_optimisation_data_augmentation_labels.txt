1	Loss function
2	Because the model produces T output positions
3	but doesn't know in advance how many of
4	those positions correspond to each character.
5	It is trained with connectionist temporal classification
6	For a target label sequence y and an output
7	sequence of length-T alignment, CTC defines
8	the probability of y as the sum, over every
9	possible length-T alignment path pi that
10	collapses (by merging repeated consecutive
11	symbols and then removing blanks) to y.
12	This is what allows the model to be trained
13	from only "this line says X" labels, with no
14	annotation of where each character sits inside the
15	image.
16	Optimisation
17	Parameters are maintained by keeping a per-parameter
18	running average of squared gradients v and scales
19	each update by its inverse square root with an added
20	weight-decay term for regularisation. A learning-rate
21	scheduler halves the learning rate whenever validation
22	loss fails to improve for a fixed number of consecutive
23	checks, and training stops early once that patience
24	is exceeded.
