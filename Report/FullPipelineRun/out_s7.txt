  7.3 The matched reference — real vs synthesised, same words
This is the fairest single comparison in the whole report. The same held-out IAM sentences are read twice by the same recogniser: once as the author's real handwriting, once as this pipeline's synthesis of those identical words. It separates "the recogniser finds synthetic strokes hard" from "the recogniser finds this author hard".
Source of the same words | character accuracy | word accuracy
REAL handwriting | 89.0% | 70.5%
SYNTHESISED by this pipeline | 81.6% | 46.7%
gap | -7.4 pp | -23.8 pp
---
Read the gap, not the absolute number. A synthesis that loses only a few points of character accuracy against real handwriting for the same words is reproducing the letterforms; word accuracy falling much further than character accuracy means the failures are concentrated in word boundaries and spacing rather than in the letters themselves.
