# New offline subsystem evidence, 1 November 2026

The user authorised use of existing datasets, photographs, profiles and software
testing for report explanations. No pipeline source, checkpoint or profile was
modified. No training or physical motion was performed.

## Facts and limitations

- Native Owen01 produced 20 crops for 20 labelled rows. Count agreement is only
  a gross splitting check. Its first two crops and actual seams are shown.
- Twelve additional cases use images reduced to fit 1600 by 1600 pixels. Digital
  gradient/rotation variants are synthetic, not new measured captures. All counts,
  including failures, appear in the appendix. The reduced Owen first-line crop
  failed; the glyph extraction illustration instead uses a separately identified
  original archive crop visually matched to its original transcript.
- Three actual 28 October camera-roll images show the same sparse three-row page
  under different shadows and presentation. The unchanged pixel arrays were
  passed to automatic axis selection and segmentation. Their output counts were
  4, 3 and 28. No orientation failure was silently corrected. No lux or physical
  angle was measured. The figures retain the failed orientation explicitly.
- Candidate scores use the joint reader and the selected 11-class width-0.17
  model from Downloads, with preprocessing explicitly set to 0.17. The three
  pre-repair trials explain scoring; they are not the full server search, a
  matched ink/shape ablation or independent stylistic validation.
- Four final profiles generate the same text with the same seed; emitted G-code
  is parsed back for inspection. A separate 9-fixture check disables joining and
  closure to compare vertices. It checks serialization after placement, not
  physical positioning or independent correctness of the placement function.
- Small independent checks: 100 full component-partition matches against BFS;
  20 optimal seam costs against exhaustive enumeration; repeated-label CTC loss
  against 1024 enumerated paths; all 20 corresponding logit derivatives against
  central differences. Their scope is explicit in the report.
- Instrumenting synthesis for a joining illustration produced no actual joins in
  the selected 150 profile/string trial. No artificial passing pair was created;
  the joining illustration remains pending. The instrumented function was restored
  immediately and stored profiles were not changed.
- The existing Owen robot-page photograph is shown separately from new software
  renders. It is not claimed to be a new hardware experiment or the same text/job.
