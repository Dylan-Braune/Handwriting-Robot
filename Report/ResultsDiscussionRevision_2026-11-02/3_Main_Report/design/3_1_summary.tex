\subsection{Design summary}
\label{sec:des-summary}
This section summarises the project design tasks and how they were implemented (see Table~\ref{tab:des-summary}). The tasks follow the deliverables and student design work in the approved Project Proposal. The qualification tests in Results assess numerical compliance.

\begin{table}[!htbp]
\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.24\linewidth}|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.23\linewidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{Deliverable or task}&\textbf{Implementation}&\textbf{Completion and report location}\\\hline
Investigation of methods& Handwritten text recognition, author recognition, image conditioning and reproduction methods were compared, and design choices were made.&Completed, seen in Literature Review, Sections~\ref{sec:des-class}, \ref{sec:des-reproduction}.\\\hline
Page capture and image conditioning&A fixed USB camera module captures handwritten pages, and an image-processing pipeline conditions the page before segmentation &Completed, seen in Sections~\ref{sec:des-camera} and~\ref{sec:des-seg}.\\\hline
Separation of handwritten text&The segmenter creates boundaries between lines while preserving the quality of the text, to produce line crops.&Completed, seen in Sections~\ref{sec:des-seg}, \ref{sec:des-alignment}.\\\hline
Text and writer classifiers&Separate networks recognise line text and identify the writer from convolutional features, bidirectional LSTM laters and CTC decoding. &Completed, seen in Section~\ref{sec:des-class}.\\\hline
Network training implementations& Training and backpropagation implemented from first principles calculates the network outputs, loss derivatives and parameter updates to train the networks. &Completed, seen in Sections~\ref{sec:des-training}--\ref{sec:des-netverify}.\\\hline
Writer-specific trajectory maps and storage&Character glyphs are extracted, converted to pen paths are stored with each writer's style features.&Completed, seen in Sections~\ref{sec:des-alignment}-\ref{sec:des-profile}.\\\hline
Handwriting synthesis and G-code&The synthesiser lays out glyphs, connected portions of the sentence, selects a candidate and converts its paths to machine coordinates.&Completed, seen in Sections~\ref{sec:des-layout}-\ref{sec:des-gcode}.\\\hline
Gantry, pen mechanism and motion control&The gantry combines a two-axis belt drive, switched pen lift, end stops and calibration to create precise cartesian movements.&Completed, seen in Section~\ref{sec:des-hardware}.\\\hline
Processing and integration&Web server display connects capture, processing, stored profiles and execution.&Completed, seen in Section~\ref{sec:des-integration}.\\\hline
Subsystem verification&Numerical checks and offline image and path experiments verify the stages. Results report the integrated qualification.&Completed, seen in Sections~\ref{sec:des-netverify}, \ref{sec:des-segverify}; Appendix~\ref{app:des-visual-tests}.\\\hline
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Design summary.}
\label{tab:des-summary}
\end{table}
