\subsection{Design summary}
\label{sec:des-summary}
This section summarises the project design tasks and how they were implemented (see Table~\ref{tab:des-summary}). The tasks follow the deliverables and student design work in the approved Project Proposal. The qualification tests in Results assess numerical compliance.

\begin{table}[!htbp]
\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.24\linewidth}|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.23\linewidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{Deliverable or task}&\textbf{Implementation}&\textbf{Completion and report location}\\\hline
Investigation of methods&Compared recognition, conditioning and reproduction approaches.&Completed; Literature Review; Sections~\ref{sec:des-class}, \ref{sec:des-reproduction}.\\\hline
Page capture and image conditioning&The camera supplies pages for illumination correction, ink extraction and orientation correction.&Completed; Sections~\ref{sec:des-camera} and~\ref{sec:des-seg}.\\\hline
Separation of handwritten text&The segmenter separates lines and assigns their ink. Known transcripts then guide glyph localisation.&Completed; Sections~\ref{sec:des-seg}, \ref{sec:des-alignment}.\\\hline
Text and writer classifiers&Separate networks recognise line text and identify the writer from convolutional features.&Completed; Section~\ref{sec:des-class}.\\\hline
Network training implementations&The numerical and framework routes train the same network architectures.&Completed; Sections~\ref{sec:des-training}--\ref{sec:des-netverify}.\\\hline
Writer-specific trajectory maps and storage&Aligned glyphs are traced, filtered and stored with each writer's style measurements.&Completed; Sections~\ref{sec:des-alignment}--\ref{sec:des-profile}.\\\hline
Handwriting synthesis and G-code&The synthesiser lays out glyphs, selects a candidate and converts its paths to machine coordinates.&Completed; Sections~\ref{sec:des-layout}--\ref{sec:des-gcode}.\\\hline
Gantry, pen mechanism and motion control&The gantry combines a two-axis belt drive, switched pen lift, end stops and calibration.&Completed; Section~\ref{sec:des-hardware}.\\\hline
Processing and motion integration&Server connects capture, processing, stored profiles and execution.&Completed; Section~\ref{sec:des-integration}.\\\hline
Subsystem verification&Numerical checks and offline image and path experiments verify the stages. Results reports the integrated qualification.&Completed; Sections~\ref{sec:des-netverify}, \ref{sec:des-segverify}; Appendix~\ref{app:des-visual-tests}.\\\hline
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Design summary.}
\label{tab:des-summary}
\end{table}
