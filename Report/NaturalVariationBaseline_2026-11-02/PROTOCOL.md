# Matched natural-writing baseline protocol, 2 November 2026

Measure genuine same-writer, same-token variation in the already reviewed original
reference banks and compare physical robot words on exactly matched reference sets.
No new score-based data selection, tolerance changes or handwriting transformations.
Preserve earlier results and all report sources. Do not run models or hardware.

Use all 11 writers and the two fixed normalisations from the all-author whole-word
study. Inputs are its frozen accepted masks and labels. Check for identical raw
reference-crop duplicates. Self-comparisons are forbidden. Tokens are case-sensitive
and punctuation-sensitive. No new reference crops are added for a favourable result.

Primary comparison is cross-page matched reference-bank fit. For each genuine probe
word, use every accepted same-writer same-token reference from OTHER source pages.
Compare the genuine probe with that bank and take its highest F. Compare each robot
word with exactly that same bank and take its highest F. Both sides have identical
bank cardinality and scoring. Average over genuine probe occurrences within a word
and then over physical words within each writer. Weight writers equally in the main
aggregate. Keep all low scores. A word without at least two source pages is outside
the cross-page comparison and must be explicitly listed.

Also retain two secondary checks. (1) Leave-one-occurrence-out, permitting different
occurrences on the same page. (2) Mean over all probe-reference pairings, without
best-reference selection, using identical reference sets for natural and robot.
Report all these results, not only the most favourable one.

Keep both absolute scores visible. A robot/natural score ratio is a descriptive
relative-to-baseline ratio, can exceed 100 percent and is not an accuracy, a Pearson
correlation, an upper bound, a count of acceptable words or proof of the proposal's
85-percent specification. Never cap ratios or call the measured baseline a ceiling.
The main whole-word-height score's tolerance remains 4.8 pixels of a 48-pixel total
word height. The line-x-height score remains separately labelled.

Save individual original-original comparisons, every matched fold, per-word and
per-writer results, coverage and visual examples. Validate natural pair scores with
the full raster implementation and independent nearest-neighbour calculations.
Resampling word types can provide a descriptive range conditional on these banks,
not a confidence claim about unseen writers or human acceptability.
