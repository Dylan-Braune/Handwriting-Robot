0	Line Separation and Preprocessing
1	The input to this stage is a raw photo of
2	a handwritten image, and the output is a set
3	of individual line images plus a label for each region
4	as either 'text' or 'non-text' (diagrams, tables
5	or sketches). It runs as a fixed sequence
6	of classical image-processing stages, not a learned
7	model.
8	MESS
9	1. Page Localisation
10	The photo is converted to grayscale and Gaussian-
11	blurred to suppress paper texture and camera noise,
12	then a global threshold is chosen automatically using
13	Otsu's: for a candidate threshold t, pixels are split
14	into a "below" class and an "above" class, and
15	Otsu's criterion picks the t that maximises the
16	between-class variance. Combined with a saturation
17	cap (to exclude coloured background clutter), this
18	produces a rough "paper vs. not-paper" mask.
19	Morphological closing and a flood-fill hole-fill (marks
20	everything reachable from the image border as
21	background, and anything not reached that's inside
22	the mask is a hole to be filled).
