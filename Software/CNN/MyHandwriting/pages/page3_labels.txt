0	5. Line Grouping
1	Each surviving ink blob is treated as a connected
2	component with a bounding box and centroid.
3	Components in a plausible single-line size range
4	are chained left to right. The next candidate to its
5	right is chosen by minimising a cost that
6	combines the horizontal gap between them and
7	vertical deviation. Chains that end up close
8	together in their local vertical position across their
9	shared horizontal span get merged, since a single
10	line is often split into multiple chains by a stray
11	gap. Dots, ascenders, descenders or multi-line
12	components are assigned afterward by finding the
13	nearest line's locally-fitted baseline curve.
