1	adaptive thresholding: for each pixel, a local mean is
2	computed over a surrounding window and the pixel is
3	classified as ink if it's darker than the local mean
4	by more than a fixed margin. A colour-mask
5	separately flags red/coloured ink so it can be
6	excluded from the black/blue ink pass, and
7	small isolated blobs are removed.
8	3. Deskew
9	The camera is rarely square to the page, so a
10	rotation angle is estimated by searching over a range
11	of candidate angles, rotating the binary ink mask
12	by each candidate, picking the angle that maximises
13	the variance of that profile. The chosen angle
14	is applied to the whole page through an affine
15	rotation before any line grouping happens, since it
16	assumes horizontal lines. A second pass fixes any
17	errors from the first one.
18	4. Rule-line and border cleanup
19	Ruled notebook lines are detected as long, thin,
20	low-height connected runs (measured through
21	run-length encoding along each row) and removed,
22	but only after checking that removing them wouldn't
23	also delete a component that's actually a real hand-
24	drawn rule. This is done by comparing local ink
25	darkness, since printed/faint rules are consistently
26	lighter than pen strokes.
