0	Chosen Baseline Model
1	The chosen conversion model is the
2	Unbiased Converted Measurement model
3	in the Lerro and Bar-Shalom 1998 paper.
4	Using a standard conversion model
5	(such as simple cos and sin conversions)
6	a conversion bias is introduced which
7	shows the converted values to be non
8	gaussian. The 1998 UCM model removes
9	this conversion bias, and is proved to
10	be exact and statistically consistent
11	in the 1998 paper.
