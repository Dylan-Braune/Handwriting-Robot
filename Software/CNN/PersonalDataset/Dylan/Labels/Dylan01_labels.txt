The input to this stage is a raw photo of
a handwritten image, and the output is a set
of individual line images plus a label for each region
as either 'text' or 'non-text' (diagrams, tables
or sketches). It runs as a fixed sequence
of classical image-processing stages, not a learned
model.
The photo is converted to grayscale and Gaussian-
blurred to suppress paper texture and camera noise,
then a global threshold is chosen automatically using
Otsu's: for a candidate threshold t, pixels are split
into a "below" class and an "above" class, and
Otsu's criterion picks the t that maximises the
between-class variance. Combined with a saturation
cap (to exclude coloured background clutter), this
produces a rough "paper vs. not-paper" mask.

everything reachable from the image border as
background, and anything not reached that's inside
