- Jeremy Cronin
- served 7 years in prison for opposing apartheid
- written in Sesotho
- It centres around the African spirit of Ubuntu: Togetherness
- A person's essential being is shaped by the
people around them.
- Theme of human solidarity.
- The speaker is sharing with the reader a personal
experience. This is evidenced by the personal
pronouns "I" and "My".
- The speaker is not simply telling a story - they are
involved in the situation.
- In this case, the speaker is a prisoner in a prison,
sharing their experience.
- The poem explores the theme of wordless communication
and how the speaker and a friend express
solidarity through a minor, silent exchange.
- The poem is autobiographical.
- The speaker seems to be in a cell, given that he
has to use the reflection off his mirror to see down
the passage.
