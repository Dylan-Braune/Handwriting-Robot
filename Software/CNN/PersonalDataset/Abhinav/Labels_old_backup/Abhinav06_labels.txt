Scenario 1: one-direction acceleration
~
When the acceleration is between
0.01 and 1, the baseline performs
considerably better than the EKF.
During this time, the velocity
increases steadily. While it is
eventually capped at the maximum
speed achieved by the bird, the
initial error is to significant
for the filter to recover. At the
low acceleration, the changes in
velocity are too miniscule for it
to meaningfully affect performance
of the EKF. At high acceleration,
the object reaches it maximum
speed too quickly
for the EKF to be wrong
for a long time, so it
reaches the constant velocity state
