0	-> Why I can't just compare # two means directly
1	- Naive comparison techniques (just checking if confidence intervals
2	around each filter's mean overlap) are FLAWED because UCM and
3	DUCM aren't independent of each other
4	- in my setup, both filters are fed the exact same simulated measurement
5	sequence in each run -> TO REMOVE NOISE FROM CONFOUNDING THE
6	COMPARISON -> BUT it means the 2 filters' per-run errors are
7	correlated w/ each other
8	- can't treat them as 2 separate independent samples and compare
9	their means w/ a normal two-sample test - the shared randomness
10	would corrupt the comparison
11	* LOOK AT DIFFERENCE NOT RAW VALUES
12	- Instead of comparing C^(1) against C^(2) directly, the OT works w/ the
13	per-run difference:
14	Delta_i = C_i^(2) - C_i^(1)
15	where C_i^(j) is just "whatever cost/error metric you're measuring
16	for filter j on run i" - eg. squared position error, NEES for UCM
17	vs. DUCM on a single run
18	- how it fixes the correlation problem: even though C_i^(1) and C_i^(2) are
19	correlated w/ each other w/i a run (same measurement noise realisation),
20	the difference Delta_i from run i is independent of Delta_k from a different
21	run k, because diff runs use fresh, independent random noise. So
22	you end up w/ N independent samples (Delta_1, Delta_2, ..., Delta_N) but you can
23	do ordinary statistics on.
