17	8. How do I know if any of this actually works? (Verification theory)
18	- RMSE answers: how far off is my estimate, on average -> it only tells
19	you about accuracy
20	- NEES answers: is my filter's own confidence (reported covariance P)
21	trustworthy?   ANEES - consistency
22	NEES = (xtrue - xhat)^T P^-1 (xtrue - xhat)
23	Why does this have a known distribution? Because if the filter is
24	truly consistent - its errors really are Gaussian with covariance P
25	- then this expression is exactly the sum of squares of nx standard
26	normal variables, which by definition follows a chi-sq. distr. with
27	nx degrees of freedom. So NEES gives you a statistical test, not just
28	a number: if NEES values are wildly outside chi-sq. bounds - filter is
29	lying about its uncertainty, even if RMSE looks fine.
