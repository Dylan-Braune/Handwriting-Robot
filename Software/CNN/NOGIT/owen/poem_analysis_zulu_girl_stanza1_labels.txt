0	The Zulu girl
1	Stanza 1
2	The setting is established here.
3	We are in a field on a farm, upon which our Zulu
4	girl (and many others) is ploughing. (A hoe is a
5	tool used for ploughing fields)
6	It is incredibly hot, as the acres of the farm
7	are described as "hot red" and "smouldering"
8	(Metaphor).
9	If something smoulders, it is slightly lit
10	and about to burst into flame.
11	(Don't let the odd syntax here confuse you.)
12	The reference to the group of workers as a
13	"gang" reflects their lack of individuality.
14	They are seen as an identity-less collective,
15	where the individual identity is neglected.
16	Plying labour - doing work.
17	They seem almost like a group of prisoners.
18	The woman "flings" down her hoe, in act
19	of rebellion or defiance.
20	She does not simply set it down. Picture the
21	action.
22	She, in this moment, neglects her subjection
23	to manual labour, for the purpose of tending
24	to her young child.
25	Her child is portrayed as vulnerable
26	certainly out of place in the hot fields,
27	and tormented by flies.
28	Diction: Tormented: Harassed by something
