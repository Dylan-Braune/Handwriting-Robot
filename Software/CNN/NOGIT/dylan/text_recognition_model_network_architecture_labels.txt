6	Text-recognition model: architecture, training, and inference
7	1. Network architecture
8	The recognition model is a hybrid convolutional-
9	recurrent model. The input is a single-channel line
10	image resized to a fixed height and width. It passes
11	through three convolutional stages, each stack of
12	three convolutions followed by batch
13	normalization and a ReLU nonlinearity, with channel
14	counts increasing 1 to 32 to 64 to 128 across the
15	three stages (12 layers in total). A 2 by 2 max-pool
16	follows the first and second stages only, halving
17	both spatial dimensions each time. An adaptive
18	max-pool collapses whatever height remains down
19	to exactly one row.
20	The sequence is fed into a two-layer
21	bidirectional LSTM with hidden size 256 per
22	direction. At each timestep the forward and
23	backward hidden states are concatenated into a
24	single-dimensional vector, which is passed through
25	a single layer projecting to N+1 logits, followed
26	by a log-softmax to produce a probability
