This handwriting robot was designed to
identify the words and writer of any piece of
handwritten text, then reproduce new
sentences in that same persons style using a
gantry and pen. Over 13 different writers were
used to train and test the system, each
contributing several pages so the network could
learn how they loop their letters, slant their
lines and space their words.
