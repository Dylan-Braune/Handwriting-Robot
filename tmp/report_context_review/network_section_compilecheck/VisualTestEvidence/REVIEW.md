
## Redraw for Appendix 4, 2026-11-01

All twelve included figures were redrawn to the Appendix 4 typography rules
and for legibility. Nothing about what they show changed, except where noted
for T56.

- **Drawn at the printed size.** Each figure is authored 6.30 in wide, which
  is the report's `\textwidth` of 455.24 pt, and every include was changed
  from `width=0.9x\linewidth` to `width=\linewidth`. With the scale factor at
  one, a point in the figure is a point on the page, so the 8 pt floor holds
  on paper and not only in the source.
- **Text at 8 pt or above, one font throughout.** No call sets a bare font
  size any more; all go through a helper that carries the floor. Labels that
  contain a subscript or a superscript have a 9.5 pt floor so the smaller
  glyph also clears 8 pt, and the shrink fraction was raised to 0.85.
- **Caption below the figure, not inside it.** The figures no longer carry
  their own caption strip or provenance line, so the PDF cropping the earlier
  pass applied is gone; the caption text is kept in `CAPTIONS.md`.
- **Crops enlarged.** This was the main complaint. A line crop is about
  fifteen times wider than it is tall. Long lines are now stacked in bands
  (T25, T36, T49), blank margin above and below the ink is dropped (T40, T41,
  T43), windows are cut to an aspect that suits a printed panel, and image
  panels are drawn at equal aspect so handwriting is no longer stretched to
  fill its box. T40's crops in particular went from a thin band of ink in a
  mostly empty box to filling the panel, which is what makes the dilation
  kernel visible at all.
- **Layout.** Column headings in T45 are stated once instead of on every row;
  T25's row heights are computed from the data and its panels no longer
  collide with their titles; T32's input line spans the full width; T27's
  three cases share one window shape so they align; T52 and T56 lost their
  overlapping labels.
- **T56 changed what it shows.** Its fourth profile was the numbered IAM
  profile `150`, which printed as a bare "150" beside Owen, Dylan and
  Yeukita. It is now Abhinav, the fourth named personal profile. Abhinav
  spends 15.971 s drawing, so his dwell share is 52.7% and the four-job range
  is 52.7-65.1%, not the 61-65% quoted before. Section 3.6.7 was updated to
  match, and the figure is generated by `vt_motion.py` rather than replotted.
