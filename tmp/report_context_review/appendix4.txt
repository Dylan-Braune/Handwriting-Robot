In this section you have to provide a summary of in formation from the literature that was

the literature study in the first semester report. 
 
 
Literature sources to be consulted include journal articles from high quality technical journals 
(e.g. IEEE, Springer, Elsevier or Wiley journals), technical information from good quality 
technical websites, books and standards documents. 
 
Organise this section into  
(i)  a description of the background and context (typica lly between three and five pages), 
referring to the relevant literature, and  
(ii)  a brief summary of how you applied this background (typically one to three pages).  
 
The former is a structured summary of what other pe ople have done before you that supports 
your work, including (but not limited to) how other s have solved similar or related problems. 
The latter summarises what has been learnt in the l iterature study and describes how you 
applied what you learnt from literature.  
 
One way to approach a literature study is to see this as an argument where you guide the reader 
through relevant literature. You want to show your reader, by referring to several examples (i.e. 
by citing the relevant literature), what others hav e done and how this applies to your specific 
project, and perhaps where shortcomings exist that you needed to address with your project. 
 
A good literature study will not be an article-by-article summary of the material that you read, 
but will merge the material into a unified view of the literature that you consulted . To see what 
a good literature study looks like, look at the int roduction section of (almost) any article 
published in an IEEE journal. Review articles (for example, like those that often appear in the 
Proceedings of the IEEE) usually contain particularly good examples of literature studies. 
 
You should give at least six references to articles in technical journals (often IEEE journals, but 
you may also include other technical journals), and  perhaps further references to other 
material.

The Approach section answers the questions, WHY and HOW? In this section you need to tell
