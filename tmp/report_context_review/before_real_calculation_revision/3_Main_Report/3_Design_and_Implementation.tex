The Cartesian plotter constrains the workspace, pen state and motion required from the software. The design therefore starts with hardware, then follows page segmentation, text/writer classification and handwriting reproduction, before describing their integration.

Established methods are referenced where introduced. The coordinate transforms, geometric heuristics and combined selection criteria are design choices developed for this system; their equations and worked examples are derived here. Numerical examples are calculations unless explicitly identified as recorded measurements. Implementation details are available in the submitted CleanPipeline3 source, with occasional module pointers provided below.\par
