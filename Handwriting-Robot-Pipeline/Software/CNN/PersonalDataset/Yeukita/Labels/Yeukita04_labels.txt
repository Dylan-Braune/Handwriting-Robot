algorithm 2, where "better" means lower cost.
H0: 0 - algorithm 1 is NOT better th algorithm 2 (no real
improvement)
H1: 0 - algorithm 1 is better than algorithm 2
2. Compute the sample mean & standard error of the differences:
is just "on average, how much better was algorithm one across
all runs" and is "how much noise/uncertainty is in that
average" - it shrinks as you add more MC runs
3. Form the test statistic:
This is "how many standard errors away from zero is the average difference".
If the improvement is real and consistent, this no. will be large. If it's
just noise, it will hover near zero.
4. Compare against threshold u0 from the normal distr. upper tail:
1.64 for 5% sig. level
2.33 for 1% sig. level.
